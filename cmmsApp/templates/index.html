How the information connects

One day. Three useful perspectives.

iEtrack places tracker records beside employee-provided updates, so the person reviewing a report can see both the activity and its context.

01 · RECORD

Attendance & activity

Punch times, breaks and available computer activity build the day's recorded timeline.

02 · EXPLAIN

Employee context

Learning Reports, manual activity, leave and offsite records explain work the tracker alone may not show.

03 · REVIEW

Relevant reports

People with the right access review task matches, working time, productivity and exceptions by employee or period.

Reports support a conversation about work; a score or activity record does not by itself explain every task.

The bigger picture

See the work behind the hours.

A timesheet tells you how long someone worked. iEtrack helps you understand the day itself: when work began, which activities were recorded and what the employee says they worked on.

When work happens away from the keyboard, employees can add manual activity and Learning Report tasks. Leave and offsite records complete the picture, making reviews more useful for both employees and team leads.

How it works

From punch in to a useful review

The desktop tracker captures a timeline. Employees add what they worked on. Authorised reviewers explore reports alongside that context.

Attendance schedules, team assignments and account permissions shape the records each person can review.

  1. 01

    Start and track

    Employees punch in through the desktop agent. Attendance, breaks and recorded activity build a workday timeline.

  2. 02

    Record the full day

    Employees enter Learning Reports and manual activity, and can submit leave or permission requests when needed.

  3. 03

    Explore the reports

    Authorised reviewers explore attendance, leave, activity, productivity and task comparisons in filtered views.

  4. 04

    Discuss what needs attention

    Use task matches, time comparisons and attendance exceptions to ask useful questions and confirm the full context.

About iEtrack

A clearer view of the workday, from attendance to outcomes.

Developed by iEngineering Australia, iEtrack brings attendance, desktop activity and employee-reported work into one place. It helps teams understand when work happened, what was recorded and how employees describe the tasks behind it.

Employees can record leave, permissions, offsite work and Learning Reports alongside their tracked time. Authorised team leads and administrators can review relevant reports, compare records and investigate exceptions with the employee's context in view.

Access to individual and team information is controlled by assigned roles and groups.
